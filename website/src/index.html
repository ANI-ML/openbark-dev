Download

Install it like any other app.

No administrator rights needed on Windows. On Linux, the .deb and .rpm ask for your password; the AppImage doesn't. The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your computer may ask you to confirm the first time you open {{PRODUCT}}. Uninstalling keeps your visits unless you choose otherwise.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 16 GB of memory recommended.

macOS

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon. 16 GB recommended, 24 GB for the larger model. Not yet tested on a Mac.

Linux

Recent 64-bit distributions, as a .deb, .rpm or AppImage. Not yet tested.

iPhone

Planned: an iPhone app for recording in the exam room. It has not been built yet.
