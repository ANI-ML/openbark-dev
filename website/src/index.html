Open-source veterinary scribe

Your consult, written up while you finish the exam.

{{PRODUCT}} records the visit, transcribes it and drafts the medical record with open models that run on your clinic's own computer. Every line links to the part of the transcript it came from, and many mismatches are flagged for you to check. Nothing leaves the building, and nothing is final until a veterinarian signs it.

Download See every feature

Windows, macOS and Linux (tested on Windows so far). Free, Apache-2.0. Pre-alpha: try it alongside your usual records.

Why clinics use it

Quick to draft, careful enough to sign.

Private by design

Recording, transcription and drafting all happen on the computer in your clinic.

  • Recordings, transcripts and notes are encrypted on disk, with a recovery key you keep.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no cloud fallback. Every network call it can make is listed in its documentation.
  • Keep visits on a backed-up drive of your choosing.

Checkable, line by line

A draft is only useful if you can trust it, so every statement shows its evidence.

  • Every line links to the moment in the recording it came from, and many mismatched links are flagged.
  • Numbers that aren't in the transcript are flagged before you can sign.
  • Optional: download a second speech model on the Models screen and it re-listens to lines with numbers and likely drug names, and flags what it hears differently.

Built for every species

Dogs and cats, horses, herds, birds and exotics, with headings that fit each one.

  • {{TEMPLATES}} templates out of the box: new consults, rechecks, phone calls, dental, surgery, rounds and more.
  • Multi-pet visits get a separate note for each animal.
  • Notes in English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian or Dutch, and the app in five of them.

Open models

Open models, running on hardware you already own.

No subscription to a model provider and no account. The models download once, then {{PRODUCT}} works offline. A graphics card makes it much faster. Timings so far come from one desktop with an NVIDIA graphics card; laptop and Mac timings are still to come.

Writes the note

Gemma 4

Google's open model, run by llama.cpp on your graphics card or processor. E4B for most computers, 12B where there's room.

Transcribes

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3

NVIDIA's open speech model, for English and 24 other European languages. Separate open models (Silero VAD, pyannote and WeSpeaker) detect speech and label speakers as you talk.

Checks the numbers

Whisper large-v3-turbo

OpenAI's open model, an optional download, listens again to lines with numbers or likely drug names, and flags what it hears differently.

Download

Install it like any other app.

No administrator rights needed on Windows or Linux; on a Mac, a standard account may be asked for an administrator password to use the Applications folder. Uninstalling keeps your visits unless you choose otherwise.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 16 GB of memory recommended.

macOS

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon. 16 GB recommended, 24 GB for the larger model.

Linux

Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora and other recent 64-bit (x86-64) distributions, as a .deb, .rpm or AppImage. Not yet tested on Linux.

iPhone

Planned: an iPhone app for recording in the exam room. It has not been built yet.
