Privacy

{{PRODUCT}} is built so your clinic's recordings and notes stay on your own devices. This page says exactly what leaves them, and when.

Last updated 24 October 2026. ANI.ML Health Inc., Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Questions: {{CONTACT}}.

The short version

  • Recordings, transcripts and notes are made and kept on your clinic's own computers and phones. We never receive them.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't sell or share data, because it doesn't collect it.
  • Recordings never leave the device that made them, even with encrypted sync turned on.
  • If you turn on encrypted sync, only encrypted copies of transcripts and notes leave your devices. They're locked with keys that stay on your devices, so our servers can't read them.

What the app sends, and when

The {{PRODUCT}} app on a computer or an iPhone connects to the internet only for the reasons below. Each one is listed, with its exact addresses, in our network document.

  • Downloading models. When you choose a speech or note model, the app downloads it from Hugging Face. Hugging Face sees your device's internet address, like any download. Nothing about your patients is sent.
  • Your own accounts, if you connect them. If your clinic connects its own Twilio account for phone calls, calls go through Twilio under your clinic's agreement with them. Recordings of those calls are made on your computer.
  • Encrypted sync, only if you turn it on. Sync is off unless you turn it on. Then the app sends your email address to sign in with a one-time code, and encrypted copies of transcripts, notes and patient records to our sync service, hosted by Supabase. We can see that an account exists, its devices' names and platforms, and the size and timing of encrypted uploads. We can't see what's inside them.

With none of these in use, {{PRODUCT}} works fully offline.

Clinic accounts and usage counts (coming soon)

Clinic accounts are coming in a later release. A clinic will be able to create one and invite its staff. If a staff member joins and turns on "Share usage counts", their app sends daily counts to the clinic's dashboard: visits recorded, minutes recorded, notes drafted, notes signed and calls recorded, with the app version and platform. Counts only: never patient names, transcripts, notes, reasons for visits or recordings. Usage counts are kept for up to 400 days. A member can stop sharing or leave the clinic at any time.

This website

The website has no analytics and sets no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you sign in to your account at /portal, we keep your email address and the short profile you choose to fill in (name, clinic, role, country, and whether you want update emails). You can delete your account and profile from the portal at any time.

Where data is stored

Your clinical data stays where your clinic keeps it, on your own devices. Account and sync data are held by Supabase on our behalf. Sign-in emails are sent through SendGrid. Both act as our processors.

Children

{{PRODUCT}} is a tool for veterinary professionals. It isn't directed at children.

Your choices

  • Delete a visit, its recording or all your data from within the app. Deleted visits are removed permanently after 30 days.
  • Turn off sync on any device, remove a device, or delete your sync account from the app.
  • Delete your website account from the portal, or ask us at {{CONTACT}}.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Significant changes are noted in the version history.
