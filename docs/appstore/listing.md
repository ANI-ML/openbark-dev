## Description (4000)

OpenBark is a free, open-source scribe for veterinary teams. Record the visit, and OpenBark transcribes it and drafts the note for you to check and sign. Recordings stay on your iPhone.

RECORD THE VISIT, GET THE NOTE
• One tap to record in the exam room. Add the patient now or after the visit.
• Speech is transcribed on the iPhone, so it works offline and recordings never leave the phone.
• On iPhones with 8 GB of memory or more, OpenBark can write the note on the phone too. On other iPhones, your clinic computer running OpenBark writes it.
• Each speaker is labelled, so you can see who said what.

CHECK EVERY LINE
• Tap any sentence in the note to see where it came from in the transcript.
• Lines that need checking are marked before you sign.
• Ask for changes, edit any section, then copy the note into your practice software.

MADE FOR VETERINARY WORK
• Templates for wellness exams, sick visits, dentals, dermatology, exotics, emergency, surgery, euthanasia and more, plus client summaries and discharge instructions. Make any template your own.
• A dental chart for dogs and cats, drafted from what's said during the procedure. You confirm every tooth.
• Visits sorted by what needs you, and each animal's visits in one place.
• Consent is recorded with every visit, the way your clinic chooses.

FINISH AT YOUR DESK
• Sync your iPhone with OpenBark on your clinic's computers, through a computer in your clinic. Transcripts and notes travel encrypted over your own network. Recordings never sync.
• No OpenBark accounts and no servers of ours: nothing comes to us.
• Sync is a preview until its independent security review is finished. Use it with test data until then.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads and no tracking.
• Visits are stored encrypted on the device.
• The source code is public, so anyone can check what it does.

OpenBark drafts notes for a veterinarian to review. It doesn't diagnose or replace clinical judgement. Always check a note before you sign it.

OpenBark is also free for Windows, macOS and Linux at openbark.io.

Built by the ANI.ML Health team.
