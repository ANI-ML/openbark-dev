## Decisions for the product owner

- **OCR engine (item 8).** Candidates: `ocrs` (pure Rust, MIT/Apache, models downloaded once, like the speech models) or Tesseract (Apache-2.0, C++ library to ship per platform). Proposal: `ocrs`, for the same single-binary, local-first packaging as the rest.
- **Retrieval model for prior records (item 8).** The brief names EmbeddingGemma. It's released under the Gemma Terms of Use rather than Apache-2.0, so it would be a user-accepted download (like MedGemma), not preloaded. Proposal: start without embeddings (prior records go in whole or as their summary; Gemma 4's context fits most histories) and add retrieval only if histories are too long.

## Risks

- Lexical alignment can't judge paraphrase perfectly; it must err towards "check this", never towards silently passing. Thresholds are set on the fixtures and reported in the eval.
- Multi-pet notes double the drafting time; drafts run one after another on one GPU.
- Small models may confuse animals in multi-pet visits; the eval measures cross-contamination.
