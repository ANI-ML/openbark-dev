# Note quality evaluation (M2 items 1 to 3)

Same method as [M1](M1.md): `openbark eval` drafts each synthetic consult and checks the declared facts, invented numbers (a hard fail) and citations. M2 adds:
- **word flags** (item 1): statements whose content words aren't in the lines they cite, printed as `check:` lines;
- **per-animal scoring** for multi-pet visits (item 2), with a cross-contamination check;
- **a template per fixture** (item 3): a script can name its template (`template: dental`), and `--template … --force-template` drafts any fixture with any template;
- **the invented numbers listed** (`invented: /path: number`), not just counted.

Model: Gemma 4 E4B (QAT Q4_0), RTX 4080. All consults are synthetic, voiced with Kokoro (`tools/fixture-tts`).

## Item 1: claim-level alignment

On the four M1 fixtures with the SOAP template: 40 / 44 facts, 0 invented numbers, 22 word flags. Reviewing each flag:

| Kind | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong line cited**, right line suggested | 13 | Exam findings citing segment 20 when said in 21; "Arthritis" citing a line before it was said |
| **Not said, or embellished** | 5 | "Follow-up visit" as the chief complaint of an annual exam; "None provided" under differentials; "Cheese given by children" |
| False flag: a word form the folding missed | 4 | "trimming" vs "trim", "age", "increased water intake" vs "drinking more" |

The four false flags were fixed (doubled-consonant stems, `age` as filler, intake/increase/decrease folds), and units such as bpm and min no longer count as content words. No planted fabrication passed: every statement in the golden tests (`align.rs`) that wasn't said is flagged, and a missing negation always is.

### What the specialty consults found

**The number check misread some spoken numbers.** "four oh four" (a tooth), "three forty" (a time) and "point seven" were added up or lost their zero. They are now read digit by digit, as two numbers, and as 0.7. The recogniser's "four o four" (a letter o) counts too.

1. **A tenfold dose error, now caught and hinted.** The recogniser wrote "point one milligrams per kilo" as `hydromorphone.1 mg`. The model wrote **1 mg/kg**, and the number check passed it, because `.1` read as 1.
   - The number check now reads a decimal without its leading zero as a decimal (`.1` is 0.1).
   - Lines like this get a visible hint (`[heard "hydromorphone.1", likely "hydromorphone 0.1"]`); the transcript itself is unchanged.
   - On re-running, the surgery note gives 0.1 mg/kg.
2. **A drug swap.** "a sepromazine" (acepromazine) became **Xylazine** in the note. The word was glued to its dose, so no hint was offered. Glued doses are now split before matching, and a drug's first syllable heard as "a" is joined back ("a sepromazine" → acepromazine).
3. **"IV" heard as "Roman 4".** This happened in three consults; the route was then lost or written as "Roman". It now gets a hint, likely IV.
4. **The vocabulary was small-animal only.** Ceftiofur came out as "Seftiapha", flunixin as "Fluunixin", bute as "Butte". 63 farm, equine, exotics, anaesthesia and suture terms were added.
5. **Ordinals** ("fourth premolar" written as "4th") counted as invented numbers; spoken ordinals now count as said.
6. **Still open:**
   - "ASA one" was heard as "A sat one", and the model wrote "SpO2 1". The word check flags it, but nothing hints it.
   - Short claims that cite the wrong line ("Heart rate is 160 bpm" citing the presenting complaint) are flagged, but no line is suggested, because suggestions need 75% word coverage.

## Item 7: the dental chart (ADR-0021)

`openbark eval` now scores tooth charts. A script's `expected.chart` lists one fact per finding (`tooth` and `finding`, plus any of `detail`, `grade`, `mm`, `planned`; a key given as null must be null, so "fractured" with no type said must stay untyped). The scorer reports:
- **findings:** expected findings found (recall) and charted findings that match an expected one (precision);
- **teeth:** distinct teeth, per-tooth recall and precision;
- **invented teeth:** charted teeth not in any expected finding, counted as errors (target 0);
- **invented normals:** a `normal` that wasn't expected (target 0);
- **wrong values:** a charted finding on an expected tooth and finding whose type, grade, millimetres or plan is wrong ("108 extraction detail closed, expected open"), counted as errors like an invented tooth, not only as a precision miss (target 0);
- **not allowed:** findings the script forbids (`chart_not_allowed`, such as resorption after "no tooth resorption", or a periodontal stage on teeth after "stage two across the mouth");
- items without evidence, and the tooth flags the faithfulness pass must raise (`chart_flags`).

### Review fixes (2026-10-19)

A code review found ways the faithfulness pass could let a wrong chart through with no flag. Fixed on the branch, with a test for each:
- **What was said about which tooth.** Cue words, the type's words, and the grade and millimetres must be in the clause that names the tooth (its sentence, split between two teeth at "and", "but", a comma or the next tooth). Words said about another tooth in the same line raise `other_tooth` ("309 has grade one mobility and 409 is grade three" charted as 309 grade 3; swapped pocket depths; citing an extra line that doesn't name the tooth). A finding said once for two teeth in one sentence still passes ("309 is fractured and 409 too").
- **Negation.** A finding said to be absent raises `negated`: "no resorption on 307", "404 is not fractured", "no pulp exposure" for a complicated fracture, "no sectioning" for a sectioned extraction, "fracture ruled out". "Uncomplicated" rules out "complicated".
- **Plans.** An extraction or crown amputation charted as done when its clause says "may", "will", "need to", "we'll" and no word for it being done raises `planned`.
- **Conflicts on one tooth** (`conflict`): normal beside another finding, missing beside a finding done, two stages, two types or two extraction techniques. The cat's "type 2" charted as a second resorption stage beside TR4b is now flagged.
- **Kind of tooth.** A persistent deciduous tooth only on 500-800, an unerupted tooth only on 100-400 (a shape flag, and refused in the editor).
- **Name conflicts** are flagged whichever of the two teeth was charted, and the prompt hint leaves out the name that disagrees with its number.
- **The tooth reader** gives no tooth for a group with a count, a vague amount or a negation in front of it ("two of the lower incisors", "a few premolars", "none of the canines"), for a group with no side or jaw ("calculus on the premolars", unless "all"), or for "the other premolars"; "to" and a hyphen make a range only inside one quadrant ("from 108 to 208" is two teeth). It reads side and jaw after the tooth word ("the fourth premolar on the upper right") and carries them to a second name said with the first ("lower left first molar and second molar"). A group said elsewhere no longer turns an invented tooth's hard fail into a citation mismatch.
- **The mouth.** With no dentition recorded, a tooth only a dog has blocks signing until dog or cat is chosen (`choose_dentition`); a tooth outside the chosen mouth blocks it too (`not_in_mouth`). Each animal of a multi-pet visit drafts with its own species from its patient record.

### Clinical re-attack, the confirmation gate and the app's prompt (2026-10-19)

**What changed.**
1. **Drafting names no other id starts.** The draft is grammar-constrained, and the finding enum held `resorption` and `resorption_type`: the model could close the string after "resorption" before "_type" was considered. E4B charted "type 2 replacement resorption" as a resorption stage 2 that way. The drafting grammar and prompt now call the stage `resorption_stage`; likewise `sectioned_closed`, `crown_root_uncomplicated`, `crown_root_complicated` and `root_fracture`, since `closed`, `uncomplicated_crown`, `complicated_crown` and `root` started other detail ids. Drafts are read back into the finding table's ids before anything else sees them (`dental::DRAFT_NAMES`, `rename_for_drafting`), so `chart.json`, saved notes, edits, the checker and the PDF are unchanged. A test fails if a new id starts another. On 12B this alone took the cat consults from 7/9 and 7/10 to 8/9 and 10/10.
2. **`grade` before `detail`** in the tooth finding schema (the property-order trial the ADR left open). The stage 4 sub-stage is a detail that depends on the stage; with the grade written first, E4B writes 4b as `crown_worse` instead of leaving it null. This fixed the 307 wrong value.
3. **The finding guide gives each plain finding its words**: "normal (normal, fine, healthy), missing (missing, absent, not present), unerupted (unerupted, not erupted, has not erupted)…", from the cue lists in `chart.json`. "404 looks fine" was charted as `other`; it is now `normal`.
4. **The negation rule says what isn't a negation**: "A tooth said to be missing or not yet erupted has that finding". "304 hasn't erupted yet" was skipped as a finding said to be absent.
5. **The extraction guide line** adds "a tooth said to be sectioned is `sectioned_closed` even when also said to be closed" (AVDC's XS is a closed extraction too). "208 is sectioned and extracted, closed" had become a closed extraction.

**Tried and not shipped.**

- "A resorption stage and type said together are two items" in the one-item-per-finding rule: 74/88.

**Species from a breed (follow-up).** The dental e2e draft for Coco (no species entered) had "Species: Yorkshire terrier". The model fills Patient as said, and the rules forbid guessing the species, so a breed said alone went into Species, and the word check passed it because the breed was said. Changing the drafting rule fixed the species but moved the chart by three findings (any prompt change does), so the fix is after drafting, from a documented table instead (`engine/os-engine/src/breeds.rs`): when Species is empty or holds a breed, and that breed names exactly one of dog or cat both in the value and in its cited line, Species becomes Dog or Cat with the breed's citations (the breed moves to an empty Breed). Unknown breeds and other animals' breeds (Arabian, Himalayan, Rex, Shetland) fill nothing. The faithfulness pass accepts Species dog or cat when its cited line names one of that species' breeds, and flags a breed written as the species. ## Speakerphone (0.1.3, ADR-0026)

The owner's report: with a phone on speaker beside the computer, the client's voice transcribed poorly; on the Review screen every line was "Speaker 1" and the client's turns came out as clipped fragments. Decisions and mechanism: [ADR-0026](../adr/0026-phone-on-speaker.md); for vets: [Recording a consult, A client on a phone on speaker](../guide/recording.md#a-client-on-a-phone-on-speaker).

**Fixtures.** Four new scripts in their own category (`category: speakerphone`, reported as `TOTAL speakerphone`): `speaker-diabetic-callback` (client 18 dB below the vet), `speaker-dropoff-lameness` (22 dB), `speaker-dental-estimate` (15 dB) and `speaker-rabbit-history` (25 dB), 43 client turns and 55 vet turns, with a different pair of Kokoro voices in each. A script's `phone:` key makes `tools/fixture-tts` render that speaker through a simulated phone: 300 to 3400 Hz (4th-order Butterworth), down to 8 kHz with 8-bit mu-law companding and back, a fast 3:1 compressor and soft clipping (the phone's level control and loudspeaker), a Schroeder room reverb of about 0.5 s at a direct-to-reverberant ratio of -3 dB, then the level below the vet's; the vet gets a light touch of the same room (+12 dB direct), and pinkish background noise runs 45 dB below the vet. The WAVs are regenerated (`--cache <dir>` keeps the voiced lines between renders); `--no-phone`, `--phone-drr` and `--phone-level` make control variants. The eval transcribes these four with "Phone on speaker" on, as the app would.

**Scoring per speaker.** `packages/eval/score_speakers.py` aligns the whole transcript with the whole script word by word, so a word lost at a segment boundary counts against the speaker who said it, and reports per speaker: words captured (recognised right), WER, the share of speech time inside any segment, segments per script line, turns dropped (fewer than half their words captured) and words under that speaker's own label. Numbers below are the live path (`openbark record --file`), which recordings and imports both use.

**Where the speech was lost (0.1.2).** In voice detection. Only 7% of the client's speech time fell inside any segment; every client turn was dropped (43 of 43) and 2.2% of the client's words reached the transcript, against 96.4% of the vet's. Lowering the detector's threshold from 0.5 to 0.35 only raised coverage to 16%. The few client fragments heard were all under a second, so the live tracker never started a second speaker: one label for everything, as the owner saw. The batch `transcribe --diarize` path gave the same numbers.
