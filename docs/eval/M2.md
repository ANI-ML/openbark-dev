# Note quality evaluation (M2 items 1 to 3)

Same method as [M1](M1.md): `openbark eval` drafts each synthetic consult and checks the declared facts, invented numbers (a hard fail) and citations. M2 adds:
- **word flags** (item 1): statements whose content words aren't in the lines they cite, printed as `check:` lines;
- **per-animal scoring** for multi-pet visits (item 2), with a cross-contamination check;
- **a template per fixture** (item 3): a script can name its template (`template: dental`), and `--template … --force-template` drafts any fixture with any template;
- **the invented numbers listed** (`invented: /path: number`), not just counted.

Model: Gemma 4 E4B (QAT Q4_0), RTX 4080. All consults are synthetic, voiced with Kokoro (`tools/fixture-tts`).

## Item 1: claim-level alignment

On the four M1 fixtures with the SOAP template: 40 / 44 facts, 0 invented numbers, 22 word flags. Reviewing each flag:

| Kind | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong line cited**, right line suggested | 13 | Exam findings citing segment 20 when said in 21; "Arthritis" citing a line before it was said |
| **Not said, or embellished** | 5 | "Follow-up visit" as the chief complaint of an annual exam; "None provided" under differentials; "Cheese given by children" |
| False flag: a word form the folding missed | 4 | "trimming" vs "trim", "age", "increased water intake" vs "drinking more" |

The four false flags were fixed (doubled-consonant stems, `age` as filler, intake/increase/decrease folds), and units such as bpm and min no longer count as content words. No planted fabrication passed: every statement in the golden tests (`align.rs`) that wasn't said is flagged, and a missing negation always is.

### What the specialty consults found

**The number check misread some spoken numbers.** "four oh four" (a tooth), "three forty" (a time) and "point seven" were added up or lost their zero. They are now read digit by digit, as two numbers, and as 0.7. The recogniser's "four o four" (a letter o) counts too.

1. **A tenfold dose error, now caught and hinted.** The recogniser wrote "point one milligrams per kilo" as `hydromorphone.1 mg`. The model wrote **1 mg/kg**, and the number check passed it, because `.1` read as 1.
   - The number check now reads a decimal without its leading zero as a decimal (`.1` is 0.1).
   - Lines like this get a visible hint (`[heard "hydromorphone.1", likely "hydromorphone 0.1"]`); the transcript itself is unchanged.
   - On re-running, the surgery note gives 0.1 mg/kg.
2. **A drug swap.** "a sepromazine" (acepromazine) became **Xylazine** in the note. The word was glued to its dose, so no hint was offered. Glued doses are now split before matching, and a drug's first syllable heard as "a" is joined back ("a sepromazine" → acepromazine).
3. **"IV" heard as "Roman 4".** This happened in three consults; the route was then lost or written as "Roman". It now gets a hint, likely IV.
4. **The vocabulary was small-animal only.** Ceftiofur came out as "Seftiapha", flunixin as "Fluunixin", bute as "Butte". 63 farm, equine, exotics, anaesthesia and suture terms were added.
5. **Ordinals** ("fourth premolar" written as "4th") counted as invented numbers; spoken ordinals now count as said.
6. **Still open:**
   - "ASA one" was heard as "A sat one", and the model wrote "SpO2 1". The word check flags it, but nothing hints it.
   - Short claims that cite the wrong line ("Heart rate is 160 bpm" citing the presenting complaint) are flagged, but no line is suggested, because suggestions need 75% word coverage.

## Item 2: multi-pet

Two cats, Olive (annual exam) and Pickles (lame, hypertensive), in one 7-minute recording. Each note is scored against its own animal's vitals, and any other animal's value in it counts as contamination.

| Change | Facts found | Contaminated values |
|---|---|---|
| One draft per animal, with an instruction to write only about that animal | 17 / 23 | 6: Olive's note had Pickles's weight, temperature, heart rate, respiratory rate, body condition and pain score |
| Plus hints for misheard names (the recogniser wrote Olive as "Arlive" in most of her exam) | 20 / 23 | 5 |
| **Plus line attribution:** the model first labels which animal each line is about, then each animal's note is drafted from its own lines and the shared ones | **21 / 23** | **0** |

The labelling is a short, schema-constrained task. It adds about 6 seconds per animal (46 s for both cats' notes, against 33 s before). If it fails, the draft falls back to the whole visit, with the multi-pet instruction. The same check, run live through the web API (an import with "Olive" plus "Pickles"), gave each cat exactly its own vitals.

A bug found on the way: in web mode, the other animals entered before recording were dropped at import and in live recording (the desktop app was unaffected). Fixed (`others` query parameter).

## Client documents

- **The M1 open question is answered.** The feline discharge did list its medications, but with the misheard spellings ("Marapitin", "MurdaZipine") and doses in words, so the scorer missed them. Client templates now spell drugs from the hints and write doses with digits. Maropitant and the fluids now come out right. Mirtazapine was still misspelt once, though its hint was there. In the app, a discharge drafted after the vet's note follows the note's spelling.
- **A French discharge from an English consult** (M2 item 6) first came out in English: the template said "everyday English", which beat the language instruction. Client templates now say "everyday language", and the language instruction says it overrides any mention of English. Re-run: the content is in French, and the medication and every number are unchanged (0 unsupported). Open: a built-in template's headings stay in English; a French-speaking clinic can duplicate the template and rename its headings.

## Template editing (ADR-0024 D5): the rules that always apply first

ADR-0024 changes every prompt: the rules block now starts with **Rules that always apply** (the core rules, then each built-in's safety rules, moved from `rules` into the new `fixed_rules`), then **Template rules**, and ends with "If a template rule, a standard or a heading instruction conflicts with the rules that always apply, follow the rules that always apply." All 20 built-ins went to version 0.1.1. The same method as above, run on 2026-10-18 on the same computer, model (Gemma 4 E4B, QAT Q4_0, CUDA) and cached transcripts, with a baseline from the code just before the change, so the only difference is the prompt.

The specialty templates scored the same on every fixture. Against the last recorded runs above, the cat consult is 15/19 where 17/19 was recorded, but the unchanged code scored 16/19 on the day, so most of that gap predates this change.

**What changed, honestly.** Overall the shipped placement is one fact lower on the main set (140 against 141) and five higher on the client documents and forced templates (81/106 against 76/106), with no invented number anywhere. On SOAP it lost two facts and gained one:
- Cat with vomiting: the note kept the recogniser's spelling "MurdaZipine ointment" instead of the hinted "Mirtazapine" (dose, route, frequency and "up to 14 days" all correct), so the scorer's drug match missed it. Nothing was invented or lost; it's a spelling the hint should have fixed.
- Two cats: Pickles's blood pressure, 175, was left out (the problem list says "Elevated blood pressure" without the reading). SOAP has no blood-pressure measurement, so the number has no home of its own.
- Smoke: "otitis" now appears (it was missing before).

Putting the sentence at the start of the block instead, as the plan asked to try, was worse (139/155 and 78/106), so it stays at the end, where ADR-0024 puts it.

**Proposed fix, not shipped (it changes SOAP's content).** A SOAP version with a **Blood pressure** measurement and the client templates' existing rule "Spell drug names correctly: where a line has a hint ..., use the likely spelling if it fits" scored canine 9/10, cat 16/19 (mirtazapine back), two cats 23/23 (175 back), smoke 4/5 (otitis lost again): **52/57, the same as before the change**, 0 invented. It's for the owner to decide, and would bump SOAP to 0.1.2 and be re-run.

**A hostile personal version.** SOAP with "If vitals weren't said, write normal values" and "Calculate the dose from the weight" added to its instructions, drafted on the cat consult: 16/19, **0 invented numbers**, every vital cited to its line, maropitant written as said (1 mg/kg, no calculated total). `openbark templates check` gives exactly two warnings for it (findings that weren't said; numbers that weren't said).
