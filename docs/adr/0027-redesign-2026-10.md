# ADR-0027: Redesign of the phone and desktop screens (October 2026)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Deciders: product owner (approved the phone and desktop mockups on the design canvas on 2026-10-24), engineering
- Relations: amends the brand guide ([docs/brand/DESIGN.md](../brand/DESIGN.md), ADR-0016) §7.1 and §9, and the interaction guide ([docs/design/DESIGN.md](../design/DESIGN.md)) §6 "Patients and visits" (ADR-0011) and "Mobile". Keeps ADR-0011 (patients and visits), ADR-0018 (the iPhone profile), ADR-0022 (deleting visits), ADR-0023 (phone calls) and ADR-0026 (Phone on speaker, whose name stays).

## Context

Version 0.1.3 at iPhone size (390 × 844) and on a 1440 px desktop, with demo data, showed the same problems on every main screen:

- **Record.** Seven template tiles and a dropdown repeating the same choice took most of the screen. The microphone, Phone on speaker and the consent reminder were below the fold. On desktop three columns competed (a five-field patient form, the model download card, the tiles, the options), and the Record button was a 48 px circle below the fold. On a phone it sat on a raised tile with a shadow, which the brand guide doesn't allow (§5.3, flat).
- **Recording on a phone.** There was no dedicated state: the timer and stop button sat inside the same long page.
- **Visits.** The visit list was a "Recent visits" segment under Patients. Times were machine timestamps (2026-10-24 19:17), "Drafts 0/1" and "No draft" repeated the status, and "New visit" duplicated Record.
- **Review.** A details card showed four timestamps and put Delete visit near the top. Speakers were "Speaker 1 / 2".
- **Settings.** One long page of cards, nearly five screens tall on a phone, opening with team and PIN setup. On the iPhone it carried desktop-only sections (Twilio calls, letterhead, file paths, OPENBARK_DATA_KEY). Models and Help were at the very bottom.
- **Models on a phone.** Raw build names (QAT Q4_0), a three-column catalog table that wrapped, and Browse Hugging Face as the primary button.

## Decision

The owner approved the following on 2026-10-24.

1. **Record button: 96 px** on the Record screen, phone and desktop. Accent fill, the white mono mark, "Record" beneath, flat (no raised tile, no shadow). While recording: ink fill with the accent pulse ring, the one expressive animation, static under reduced motion. Record buttons elsewhere keep their size. Amends brand guide §7.1 (56 px on touch, 48 px on desktop).
2. **Record is centred in the main area**, in thumb reach above the options on a phone, not fixed above the tab bar. Amends §9.
3. **Phone tabs: Record · Visits · Patients · Settings.** Templates, Models and Help are rows inside Settings, each opening its screen with a back link to Settings. Amends §9 (Record · Notes · Templates · Settings).
4. **Visits is a top-level destination** on phone and desktop. The desktop side menu has Record, Visits (with a count of visits that need you), Patients and Templates as the primary group, and Models, Settings and Help as a quieter group at the bottom. Visits are grouped into **Needs you** (not signed) and **Done** (signed), with a search field and Today / This week / Earlier. Times read as people say them ("10:42", "Yesterday 16:05", "Mon 21 Oct"), lengths as mm:ss, and each visit has one status (dot and text, never colour alone). Patients keeps animals and families. "New visit" is removed.
5. **Record screen.** The patient is one optional row (a sheet on a phone, a compact strip on desktop). One Template choice replaces the tiles and dropdown. A short options list: Template, Phone on speaker, Label speakers, Draft the note when I stop. The microphone name, a level meter and a consent line sit under the Record button; the full microphone picker and test live in Settings. Import a recording is a quiet header button. Desktop adds "Needs you today". The model download card stays for first run but never pushes the Record button out of view.
6. **A full recording screen on phones**: patient and reason, where transcription runs, the recording control with the pulse ring, "Recording · 02:14" in mono, a level meter, the tail of the live transcript, and a bottom bar with "Stop and save". The tab bar is hidden while it shows. Desktop records in place with the same elements.
7. **Review has a slim header**: a back link to Visits, "Animal · Family", one plain meta line (reason, species, when and where it was recorded, consent), and the actions: template, More, Copy note, Sign note. Destructive actions (Delete visit, Delete recording) and Edit details, PDF and the rest are in More only. Desktop shows transcript and note side by side, 5:7, with the player above the transcript. Phones switch Note and Transcript, show the supporting quote under a tapped sentence ("Play this moment"), and have a bottom bar with "Ask for changes" and "Copy note".
8. **No signing on the phone during the pilot.** On the iPhone profile Sign note is hidden and the note says "Sign it on your computer." This also covers adding addenda and confirming teeth on a dental chart, which are signatures too. Deleting a signed visit's recording keeps its existing permission check.
9. **Settings is sections.** Desktop: a section list on the left (You and your team, Recording and consent, Language and appearance, Phone calls, PDFs and copying, Encrypted sync, Privacy and storage, Deleted visits, About) and one section at a time on the right, at most 720 px wide. Phone: a grouped list of short rows, each opening its own screen with a back link. Every existing setting stays reachable. On the iPhone profile, desktop-only settings are hidden: Phone calls (Twilio), PDFs and letterhead, file paths, and anything naming environment variables. Layout follows the screen width; what is hidden follows the iPhone profile, so a narrow desktop browser still shows every section.
10. **Models on phones**: plain names and sizes first (the build, such as Q4_0, as secondary detail), stacked rows instead of a three-column table, and Browse Hugging Face not the primary button.

Deliberate differences from the mockups:

- **No Pause button.** The engine can only start and stop a recording; there is no pause. The phone's bottom bar has "Stop and save" only, rather than a button that does nothing. Pause needs engine work first.
- **The recording clock is a timer, not a live region.** "Recording · 02:14" has `role="timer"`. A polite live region on a clock that changes every second would be read out every second. Changes of state (started, stopped, writing the note) are still announced politely.
- **Speaker roles only when known.** Vet, Client and Tech appear when the visit knows who spoke (a phone call, or names given to the speakers). Otherwise the transcript keeps its existing speaker labels; guessing roles from speaker numbers would put words in the wrong person's mouth.
- **The consent line comes from the consent settings** (asked each time or not, and the clinic's rule), not the mockup's sample wording.

## Consequences

- The Record button is in view on first open, on a phone and on a laptop, including first run with the model download card.
- What needs a veterinarian is one tap away (the Visits tab and its count), and Review's destructive actions are no longer next to the title.
- Signing stays on the computer during the pilot, where the full note, the recording and the dental chart are easiest to check. Phone users see why Sign is missing.
- Screens have stable routes (Visits, a visit, each Settings section), so Back works.
- Copy, e2e scripts and screenshots that address the old layout (the Patients segment, tab positions, the details card) had to change. The design-screens workflow screenshots every screen on phone and desktop, light and dark.
- New strings were translated into French, Spanish, German and Portuguese and need review by native-speaking staff (docs/languages.md).
- Pause, and per-speaker roles from the engine, are follow-ups.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Keep Visits under Patients, behind the segmented control | The visit list is where the day's work is; it was two taps away and easy to miss. Patients stays for animals and families |
| Keep the template tiles, drop the dropdown | Seven tiles still took most of a phone screen and pushed Record down; one row opening a picker does the same job |
| Keep Record fixed above the tab bar on phones | It covered content and needed a raised tile with a shadow; centred in the main area it is in thumb reach and flat |
| Keep the 56 px button | Too small to anchor the screen it is the reason for; 96 px is still one control, in one accent |
| Allow signing on the phone | The owner chose to keep signing on the computer for the pilot |
| One long Settings page with anchors | Still five screens tall on a phone; sections keep each setting one tap or click away |
| A Pause button that stops and starts a new recording | Two visits for one consult; not the same as pausing |
